Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026

PC Tools by Wamiq respects user privacy. The tools are designed as local Windows utilities and are not built around advertising trackers, telemetry, or unnecessary data collection.

1. Apps covered

This policy applies to the PC Tools by Wamiq website and the desktop tools published under this brand, including LightClicker, CPS Lab, TimerForge, and future PC Tools by Wamiq utilities.

2. Information the apps do not collect

  • The apps do not include advertising trackers.
  • The apps do not require an account.
  • The apps do not upload your clicks, hotkeys, saved positions, timer values, or test results to PC Tools by Wamiq.
  • The apps do not collect personal profiles.

3. Local app data

Some apps may save settings locally on your device, such as preferences, hotkeys, saved positions, CPS results, timer options, window state, or first-run notices. This information stays on the local device unless you manually share or export it.

4. Update checking

The apps may check public update files to determine whether a newer version is available or whether a minimum supported version is required. LightClicker, CPS Lab, and TimerForge each use separate update files.

5. Website and downloads

The official website may be hosted by GitHub Pages and downloads may be hosted through GitHub Releases or similar services. These platforms may process standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, request time, referrer, and download activity according to their own policies.

6. Website effects

The website may use a local browser-based visual background effect. It runs in the visitor's browser and is used only for visual presentation.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when tools, features, hosting, or update behavior changes. The latest version will be published on the official website.

Summary: PC Tools by Wamiq apps are designed to run locally, avoid telemetry, and use only public update checks for version information.
